# Brain Lab — companion notebooks

Notebooks for *Brain Lab: 84 Experiments to Decode Your Biological and Artificial Intelligence* by Takeshi Ishihara.
Every Lab in the book has one notebook: record your results, compare them with published
findings, run simulations and see the AI side at work.

## How to use

**Press a badge.** The notebook opens in Google Colab in your own Google account; nothing needs
installing. Run the cells from top to bottom (Shift+Enter). To keep your changes, use
*File → Save a copy in Drive*. Cells marked "The AI side" download a small open model the first
time they run, which can take a minute.
